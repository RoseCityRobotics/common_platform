## Safety Guidelines

⚠️ **IMPORTANT SAFETY NOTICE**: Always follow these safety guidelines when operating the robot:

1. **Power Safety**: Ensure proper power connections and never exceed voltage limits
2. **Environment**: Operate in a safe, controlled environment free of obstacles
3. **Emergency Stop**: Know the location and operation of emergency stop mechanisms
4. **Maintenance**: Perform regular maintenance as outlined in the maintenance procedures
5. **Updates**: Keep software and firmware updated for optimal performance and security
